Thank you for your interest in the Yost Research Group.

I am always looking for motivated undergraduate and graduate students to join my team, although I may not always have a position available immediately. Prospective graduate students should apply to Penn State Civil and Environmental Engineering. If you plan to submit an application, you are also welcome to send me an email with your CV and a brief description of your research interests and why you are a good fit for my team.

However, please know that I receive a high volume of emails from prospective students and it is unfortunately not possible for me to reply to all of them.

Penn State undergraduates interested in research opportunities are encouraged to contact me directly via email.
