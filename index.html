Preparing for next-generation geohazards

The Yost Research Group performs geotechnical engineering research that supports building infrastructure and communities resilient to increasingly frequent and severe natural hazards.

We are passionate about engaging with communities to develop solutions to prepare for, withstand, and recover from natural hazard events. Please reach out if you are interested in collaborating with us!
